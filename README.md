# open-contexts

A fork of [OpenContexts](https://opencontexts.app/), a free, open-source window switcher for macOS.

I appreciate the effort by [Zheng Sihua](https://github.com/zshnb) in building the original
version. But what I would like in this software differs from his design and roadmap, and as such I
forked his code, and updated to fit my needs.
